LAYOUT PER PHOTO. Decide each scene's layout from what the photo actually shows, and vary framing so the video does not look like one repeated template:
- one strong hero photo (the best product shot) → fullbleed/frame/print/card, text in its clean zone;
- close-up/detail (crumb, texture, topping, hands at work) → macro, ideally followed by a wider shot (pull-out feel);
- two related photos (before/after, two products, product + place) → split/diptych;
- many products/variety → grid/collage/wall, used once as a "breadth" moment, not as a default;
- a scene where the words carry the message → slam/wall over a calmer photo;
- photos containing readable text (menu, sign, price board) → show them whole (card/print/frame), never as a background behind other text.
Use layouts with at least 3 different shapes in a 5+ scene video; never the same layout on consecutive scenes unless it is a deliberate rhythm.
PHOTO ORDER. Open with the most arresting photo (food at its best, steam, color), not the storefront. Place/craft photos in the middle (trust). Return to the best product photo for the offer. Do not follow the website's page order: a website is an information layout, a video is an emotional sequence.
PALETTE. One saturated accent per direction, sampled from the logo or the hero photo, used only for the emphasis word and key accents; secondary is a calm near-neutral that pairs with it (a tinted cream, charcoal, or a muted tone), not a second loud color. Avoid pure #000000/#FFFFFF, neon, and "AI" cyan/purple-blue gradients. Warm categories (food, bakery, family) → warm accents (amber, terracotta, honey, tomato red, olive) unless the brand colors say otherwise. Make the 3 directions' palettes clearly different (e.g. brand color / warm editorial / bold contrast).
QUALITY CHECKLIST before returning each direction:
1. Is the arc named in your head, and does scene 1 hook in outcome language?
2. Does the value land by scene 2?
3. One role and at most one emphasis word per scene?
4. Word count fits the scene time (fewer words when fast)?
5. Text placed in each photo's clean zone, off the subject?
6. Motion varied, no two identical in a row, one breather before the climax?
7. Layout varied and justified by the photo?
8. One accent color, warm/cool fitting the category?
9. Logo reveal fits the logo; the CTA is calm and clear?
10. Could any line appear unchanged in another business's ad? If yes, rewrite it.
