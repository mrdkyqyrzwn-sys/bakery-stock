A. SKILL TEXT (4,235 characters)

TITLE: נקי ומינימלי (minimal)
FEEL: one idea per frame, one accent, lots of air. The photo is the hero; type is small, exact, calm. Premium = restraint, never emptiness.

HOOK (scene 1). Frame 0 is the thumbnail: the strongest photo already composed with the headline landed by ~0.5 s. Never a logo, blank ground or fade from white. Stillness + one precise line stops the thumb. Types:
- Precise fact (only from the details): "שלושה רכיבים בלבד" / "נאפה הבוקר" / "עשוי ביד, אחד אחד" / "טיפול אחד, 40 דקות"
- Quiet question: "מה באמת צריך?" / "למה פחות עובד יותר?" / "איך נראה עור רגוע?" / "כמה זמן לוקח לעשות נכון?"
- Detail reveal (square macro, then pull-out): "תסתכלו מקרוב" / "הפרט הקטן" / "ככה זה נראה מבפנים" / "כל שכבה בנפרד"
- Pair (diptych): "חומר גלם. תוצאה" / "מהקמח לכיכר" / "יד אחת, עבודה אחת" / "הכלי והתוצאה"

SEQUENCES:
- 20 s "Object": photo/frame hook > photo/side value > object-card/square > promise-pills/diptych > offer or price-sticker/frame > contact-end.
- 30 s "Keynote": question-hook/frame > photo/square detail > photo/side craft > object-card > big-number (real only) > photo/diptych > breather photo/frame static-drift > offer/frame > contact-end.
- 30 s "Ritual" (beauty/clinic/course): photo/frame > timeline-list (3 real steps) > photo/side > promise-pills > photo/diptych > breather > whatsapp-end.
- 60 s: repeat the proof block (side > square > diptych) with new photos, add one breather every ~15 s, one date-strip or big-number mid-way; climax at 60-80%. Add photos, never new energy.

LAYOUT / MOTION / textPos:
- Hero product on a clean ground: frame, slow push-in, text in the margin.
- Texture/close-up: square, static-drift or pull-out, text outside the square.
- Person, room, wide place: side, pan toward the empty half.
- Two related photos: diptych, static-drift, one short headline.
- Busy photo: square on its calmest part, or skip it.
- 9:16: textPos top or middle; nothing in the bottom 35%. Keep one textPos for 2-3 scenes (grid feel), change only at story turns. No two identical motions in a row.

PACE, MUSIC, TRANSITIONS, LOGO:
- pace calm; medium only for retail/deals. Never fast.
- music tempoShift -8..-2; vary variant/key across directions.
- transitionFamily: crossfade for food/wellness/clinic, mask for beauty/premium products, wipe for retail/services/deals. One per direction.
- logoReveal: mask-wipe or draw-on (line logos), light-sweep for glossy marks. logoTiming slow (normal for deals). Lockup holds still 2-3 s.

PALETTE: secondary = near-neutral ground (warm off-white #F3F0EA, stone #E7E4DE, or graphite #1F2023 for a dark direction). accent = ONE saturated color from the logo or product, only on the emphasis word, thin rules and price; readable on the ground (4.5:1). No pure #FFF/#000, no second color. 3 directions: light neutral / graphite / brand tint.

COPY:
- headline 2-4 words, a fact or a quiet claim; sub 4-6 words; kicker one plain label ("הרכב", "תהליך", "מחיר").
- Nouns and verbs, no adjective stacks. Short line, then silence.
- emphasis in about half the scenes, on the fact word (number, material, time), never the brand.
- ghost: one quiet abstract noun ("דיוק", "אוויר", "חומר", "שקט"), not in any headline.
- Avoid: "יוקרתי", "פרימיום", "מושלם", "חוויה", "איכותי", superlatives, exclamation marks, English.

CATEGORY:
- Food: crossfade, warm off-white, square macro of crumb/texture, accent from crust or sauce.
- Beauty: mask, texture macro hook (cream, drop), timeline-list ritual, accent from packaging.
- Retail: wipe, one product per frame, object-card with priceDetail, price alone with lots of space.
- Services/courses/clinics: side with people and space, timeline-list "איך זה עובד", real facts as pills; no outcome promises, no before/after diptychs for medical or aesthetic treatments.
- Deals/travel: pace medium, wipe, date-strip, price-sticker in a frame; accent only on the price.

NEVER:
1. Never two accents, or the accent on more than one word per scene.
2. Never more than 3 text elements in a frame, or text on the subject.
3. Never fast pace, slam energy, or the same layout twice in a row.
4. Never let titles drift after landing; motion stays on the photo.
5. Never prove premium with adjectives; show the detail.

B. ENGINE IDEAS
- Hairline grid: a fixed column grid with 2-3 px accent rules that draw on in ~0.4 s, with photos and text snapping to it (1 px vanishes after compression).
- RTL-aware wipe/mask: the reveal travels right to left, expo-out over 0.5-0.7 s with a soft 2-4% feathered edge, and the next scene enters as the previous exits (30-50% overlap).
- Varied title entrances: per-word blur 6 px to 0 plus opacity in ~0.13 s with a 0.067 s stagger, or a per-word grey-to-ink colour fill; titles hold perfectly still once landed; exits 40-50% shorter than entrances.
- Frame-0 rule: the first frame is already composed (photo + landed headline) so the thumbnail is the hook; no fade from white or black.
- Calm camera cap: at most 1.00 to 1.06-1.08 scale per scene, ease in-out; no breathing or floating loops on text.
- Price "space halo": wide empty space around the price for premium directions, tighter for deals (more surrounding space makes a price read larger).
- Hebrew type scale: titles 4-9% of frame height, quiet lines 2.5-4%, Hebrew about 10-15% larger than Latin equivalents, slight open tracking only on the small kicker, minimum ~28 px at 1080x1920.
- Quiet sound design: a soft paper or tick sound only on wipes and the logo lockup, music 0.4 s fade-in and 1.5 s fade-out, the music thins out while the lockup holds 2-3 s.
