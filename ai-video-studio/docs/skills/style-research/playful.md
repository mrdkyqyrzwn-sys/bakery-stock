## A. SKILL TEXT

TITLE: צבעוני ושמח (playful)

1. FEEL: a cheerful sticker book popping on the beat: real photos as die-cut stickers on flat candy-color blocks. Springy and witty, never childish.

2. HOOKS (second 1: the strongest photo as sticker or blocks + a 2-4 word headline). Vary the type per direction:
- Playful question: "בא לכם משהו מתוק?", "עוד ביס אחד?", "מי צריך סיבה?", "רגע, ראיתם את זה?"
- Deadpan / mock-official: "הודעה חשובה לרעבים", "זה לא דיאטה. זה [מוצר]", "אזהרה: קשה לעצור", "בדקנו. זה עובד" (only if true)
- Craving or direct address: "מגיע לכם פינוק", "ככה נראה שישי", "את זה תרצו עכשיו", "הצבע שחיפשת"
- Occasion: "יום הולדת בלי כאב ראש", "הקינוח של הערב", "המתנה שכולם אוהבים"
- Real number + twist (supplied numbers only): "[N] טעמים. ספרנו.", "[N] שנים של חיוכים"

3. SEQUENCES (never the same layout twice in a row):
- POP & PILE 20s: question-hook/sticker → photo/blob (craving) → montage/pile (variety) → price-sticker or promise-pills/blocks → contact-end/blocks.
- OCCASION 20s: photo/blocks (occasion hook) → photo/sticker (hero) → benefit-cards/blocks → gallery3d/pile → offer or whatsapp-end.
- 30s: add paper-tags or hanging-price (pile), big-number (real stat only) and a second hero sticker before the offer.
- 60s: loop hero sticker → pile → proof on blocks; every 4-5 scenes a reset on a new color block (big-number/promise-pills/date-strip); one mid-roll mini CTA; end offer → contact-end.

4. PHOTO → LAYOUT/MOTION/TEXTPOS:
- One product on a clean background: sticker, push-in, text on the side away from the product.
- Faces, hands, results: blob framing the face, static-drift, text top or bottom, never over a face.
- Many items or variety: pile, pan-left/pan-right, textPos top.
- Busy, dark or weak photos: blocks (the color carries the scene), textPos middle on the block.
- Storefront or wide shots: blocks, pan; pull-out only for a reveal.

5. PACE / MUSIC / TRANSITIONS / LOGO:
- pace medium by default; fast for sweets, toys, parties and deals; never calm.
- music: tempoShift +3..+8 and key 0..+5 (bright lift); use +0..+3 for beauty and clinics; use a different variant in each direction.
- transitions: bounce for most cuts; iris to zoom into a hero or reveal an offer; confetti at most twice (offer, end card).
- logoReveal: flip or split-join; particles for parties and kids. logoTiming snappy (normal for beauty and services).

6. PALETTE: one bright accent + one partner (secondary), plus white and a dark ink. Never a rainbow.
- Accent opposes the photos' dominant tone so stickers pop: warm food → teal/purple; green salon → pink.
- Contrast: text on a block needs ≥4.5:1. White fails on yellow (1.4), mint (1.6), teal #2EC4B6 (2.2), orange #FF6B2C (2.8) and pink #FF4F8B (3.1). Use dark ink (#2A1240 style) on those. White is only for deep purple, blue or red (#5B2EE0 = 7.2).
- A dark or muted brand color becomes the secondary, plus one bright accent. The 3 directions use 3 different accent hues.

7. COPY:
- Rhythm short, short, twist. Headline 2-4 words with one idea; kicker 1-2 words, like a label; sub 4-7 conversational words in plural direct address.
- Humor is a wink, not a shout. It grows from the business's real details: understatement, mock officialese, a twist on a familiar phrase. At most 1-2 jokes per ad. The product stays the hero.
- emphasis = the craving or benefit word (מתוק, חגיגה, רך), never the brand name or a filler word.
- ghost = one happy word of 6 letters or fewer: יאמי, וואו, פופ, חגיגה, מתוק, קייף.
- Avoid: baby talk, stretched letters (מתוקקק), "הכי טוב בעיר", "חוויה בלתי נשכחת", dated slang, puns that need explaining.

8. CATEGORIES:
- Food, sweets, ice cream, bakery: sticker + pile, push-in on textures, price-sticker. Ghost יאמי.
- Beauty: blob for faces and results, coral or lilac accent, pace medium, confetti only at the end.
- Retail, gifts, toys: pile + blocks, paper-tags/hanging-price, fast.
- Services, courses, clinics: blocks + promise-pills/benefit-cards. Humor only in the hook, trust first, deep ink plus one accent.
- Deals, travel, events: date-strip, real big-number, offer with confetti, iris to reveal the destination, fast.

9. NEVER:
- Never more than 2 confetti moments, and never confetti on the hook.
- Never sticker on every scene; rotate all 4 layouts.
- Never write copy aimed at kids for adult buyers. Even a kids' business speaks to the parents.
- Never let a joke hide the product, the price or the CTA.
- Never more than 2 brand hues + ink, and never neon on neon.

## B. ENGINE IDEAS
- Squash-and-stretch on sticker landing (≈1.15×/0.87, volume kept), spring damping 0.4-0.6; opacity and color never bounce.
- Die-cut stickers: thick white border, hard offset shadow without blur, random tilt ±4-8°, a "peel-off" exit.
- Stickers "on twos" (pose held 2 frames) for a cut-paper feel while the background and camera stay smooth.
- Text color flips automatically by contrast: dark ink or white per block (WCAG 4.5:1 / APCA Lc60+).
- Per-word headline pop with 60-120 ms stagger and overshoot; a rounded, heavy Hebrew font with an outline (paint-order stroke).
- Pop, boing and whoosh sounds synced to sticker landings and iris, with cuts locked to the beat grid.
- Safe zone: no text in the bottom 35% or top 12% of 9:16.
- An iris "color flood" in accent → secondary as a chapter wipe; background shapes drift slowly behind the stickers.
