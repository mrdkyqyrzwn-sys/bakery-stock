## A. SKILL TEXT (4,195 characters)

```
TITLE: חם וביתי (lifestyle)
CORE: A warm, handmade story told only with the business's own photos (real hands, place, people). It should feel like a page from the owner's album, not an ad.

HOOK, SECOND 1 (strongest photo, hands/process/people first; never the logo or an empty shop):
- Moment in progress (photo/print): "הבצק כבר תפח" / "ככה מתחיל הבוקר" / "עוד לפני שפתחנו" / "ידיים בעבודה"
- Owner's voice (only if the source supports it): "התחלנו במטבח בבית" / "המתכון נשאר במשפחה" / "אותן ידיים כל יום" / "אנחנו לא רשת"
- Question to a feeling (question-hook): "מתי אכלת משהו טרי באמת?" / "מחפשים מקום שמכירים אתכם?" / "מה מריח ככה טוב?"
- Note to you (print): "שמרנו לך אחת" / "מחכה לך על הדלפק" / "הוכן בשבילך"

ARC: problem/moment > craft > people > invitation.
20s: photo/print hook > photo/stack craft detail > photo/torn people or place > montage/collage results > promise-pills/stack > contact-end/print.
30s: question-hook/print > photo/print process > photo/stack craft > photo/torn people > timeline-list "day in the life" (only supplied hours) > montage/collage > benefit-cards/stack > offer or price-sticker (supplied prices only) > whatsapp-end/print.
Founder note (30s): photo/print owner > photo/torn place > photo/stack products > paper-tags items > montage/collage customers or table > object-card hero > contact-end/print.
60s: run the craft > people block twice with new photos, add one object-card and one timeline-list mid-film, end with offer then contact-end. Never 3 scenes with the same layout in a row.

PER PHOTO: hands/process/detail: print, push-in, text away from the hands. Faces/team: torn or print, static-drift or pull-out, textPos opposite the face. Wide shop/street: torn, pan-left or pan-right. Several products: collage or stack, static-drift. Flat-lay/table: stack, tilt-up. In 9:16 prefer top/middle (Reels UI covers the bottom third); bottom only in 16:9.

PACE/SOUND/TRANSITIONS: pace calm by default, medium for retail and deals, never fast. music key 0..+3, tempoShift -6..0 (calm) or 0..+4 (medium). transitionFamily: page for story/founder/day-in-life, slide for products and retail, dissolve for beauty and emotional moments. logoReveal ink-spread or draw-on (handwritten feel), mask-wipe for retail; logoTiming slow when calm, normal when medium. Avoid particles, split-join, light-sweep.

PALETTE: take the accent from warm tones in the photos or the brand: terracotta #C8643B, honey #D9A441, olive #7A8B4F, dusty rose #C97B84. Secondary is a deep warm ink (#3B2A20) or cream (#F3E7D3). A cold brand color stays as accent, warmed by a cream/brown secondary. No neon, no pure black, no gold-on-black.

COPY: Speak as the owner: "אנחנו/שלנו" to "לך/אתכם". Headline 2-4 words, calm and concrete; sub 5-8 conversational words; kicker reads like a note label ("מהבוקר", "מהמטבח", "בשבילך"). Emphasis goes on the sensory or human word (טרי, ביד, משפחה, חם), only if true. Ghost: one warm noun of the place (בית, בוקר, תנור, פרחים, שכונה). Bullets are concrete facts. CTA is an invitation: "בואו לטעום", "קפצו לבקר", "שלחו הודעה". Avoid: superlatives (הכי, מספר 1), corporate words (פתרונות, חוויה, איכות ללא פשרות), "עסק קטן לב גדול", urgency, English.

CATEGORIES:
- Food: process > product > table; ghost תנור/בוקר; page/dissolve; honey or terracotta.
- Beauty: hands at work, calm faces, before/after only from real photos; dissolve; rose accent; "זמן שלך".
- Retail: owner's picks, paper-tags for items, stack; slide; "בחרנו בשבילך".
- Services/courses: teacher or founder face first, timeline-list of what happens in a session, "תכירו את..."; calm.
- Deals/travel: a postcard album: print/stack of place photos, date-strip, offer with the supplied price; medium; the place name as ghost.

NEVER:
1. Never fast pace or hard-cut energy; at most one big-number scene, and only with a supplied number.
2. Never put text on faces or working hands, and never crop a face with the torn edge.
3. Never invent a founding story, years, family members, "homemade" or "from scratch".
4. Never cold, neon or luxury black-gold palettes.
5. Never hard-sell or scarcity; the end is an invitation.
6. Never open on a logo, an empty room or a text-only frame.
```

## B. ENGINE IDEAS
- Warm grade on every photo: amber in the shadows, honey in the highlights, slight desaturation, plus a light paper texture and film grain (15-25% opacity so it survives compression).
- A handwriting "write-on" reveal for print captions and the kicker, using a Hebrew handwritten font such as Playpen Sans Hebrew (OFL, Google Fonts). Animated handwriting acts as a social cue and lifts attention.
- Prints land like real photos: a small random tilt (±2-4°), a soft drop shadow, a spring settle (no bounce on opacity), and tape strips or a paperclip on stack and print.
- A real page-turn transition (curl with a moving shadow) and a torn-paper reveal with a jagged animated mask, instead of a plain slide.
- Optional "stop-motion" stepping (about 12 fps hold-frames) for stack and collage builds, for the handmade feel.
- A hand-drawn underline or circle that draws itself under the emphasis word, timed to land just after the headline settles.
- Quiet foley under an acoustic bed: paper rustle on page and torn cuts, a shutter click when a print drops, a pen scratch on write-on (3-6 dB above the bed, one sound per visible action).
- A Reels safe-zone guard: auto-push text out of the bottom ~35% and the top ~14% in 9:16. Separately, give layered paper parallax depth (3-4 layers at different speeds).
