# Israeli small-business owners: marketing and video pains (angle: Israel SMB)

Research window: about 8 minutes, read-only. Hebrew queries. The search engine is US-centred and returned few Israeli forum or Facebook-group voices, so most evidence comes from Israeli price pages, official statistics and news articles. Gaps are listed at the end.

## 1. FINDINGS

1. **The market is mostly tiny businesses with tiny turnover.** 558,088 עוסקים fall under the micro-business ceiling (2023), which is 52% of all VAT-registered businesses. Of them, 430,442 are עוסקים פטורים. Average yearly turnover is about ₪30K for a פטור and ₪50K for a small מורשה. Quotes: "העוסקים שמחזור העסקאות שלהם נמוך מהתקרה של בעל עסק זעיר עומד על 558,088 עוסקים" and "כ-77% מהאוכלוסייה הרלוונטית הם עוסקים פטורים לפי חוק מע"מ (430,442 עוסקים)". The source is a document from the Tax Authority's deputy director, July 2025. https://www.capitax.co.il/Attachments/21072025-1.pdf

2. **Owners see themselves as too small for an agency, and free reach is gone.** Maariv (2021) counts "יותר מחצי מיליון עסקים קטנים ובינוניים בישראל", says Facebook and Instagram "החלו לגבות תשלום עבור פרסומים ולהקטין חשיפה", and says SMBs are "קטנים מדי ללקיחת סוכנות". https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/israel/article-881531

3. **The channels are WhatsApp first, then Facebook and Instagram, with Instagram growing.** Israeli usage in 2025: WhatsApp 99%, YouTube 97%, Facebook 88%, Instagram 81% (up 6 points from 2024), TikTok 57%. Daily use: Instagram 67%, TikTok 55%. The quote on Instagram: "עם עלייה של 6% בהשוואה לשנת 2024 עם 81% שימוש בשנה הנוכחית". Source: Israel Internet Association annual summary, reported by ICE on 26.10.2025. https://www.ice.co.il/research/news/article/1088428

4. **For many owners, WhatsApp is the whole business and also a risk.** When WhatsApp suddenly blocked small-business accounts in October 2025, the article described it as follows: "עבור רבים, במיוחד במגזרים שבהם וואטסאפ הוא כמעט התשתית הדיגיטלית היחידה, מדובר בשיתוק ממשי של הפעילות העסקית". It also says WhatsApp "שימש לא רק לערוצי פרסום אלא גם למעקב אחרי הזמנות". Source: Kipa, 26.10.2025. https://www.kipa.co.il/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA/1213878-0/

5. **Owners are shy on camera, run out of ideas and post rarely.** Quotes: "נבוכים מול העדשה, שוכחים את הטקסט, מתלבטים איך להיראות ומה להגיד, ובסופו של דבר מוותרים לחלוטין על יצירת תוכן"; "הם יודעים שהם צריכים תוכן, אבל לא מצליחים לייצר אותו בצורה עקבית"; "צילום סרטונים הוא משימה נוספת ברשימת המטלות האינסופית". The article's case example is an owner who posted 2 videos in a whole year. Source: Maariv, 23.06.2026. The article was sponsored by ShakAd, a video service that competes with Klipi. https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/consumerism/article-1336090

6. **Owners have no time and do marketing late at night.** Quotes: "אחת עשרה בלילה זו השעה שבה הוא נכנס לחשבון הפרסום ובודק מה קרה היום"; each new marketing effort "גוזלת ממנו את השעות שאמורות ללכת על ניהול העסק"; "שיחת ווטסאפ קצרה שהייתה אמורה לקחת חמש דקות מתגלגלת לשעתיים של תיאום". Source: thecyborg.co.il, 20.08.2026. https://thecyborg.co.il/magazine/when-to-hire-marketing-agency-small-business/

7. **A professional business video costs thousands of shekels.** Quotes: "מחיר סרטון תדמית בסיסי לעסק נע בין 3,000-4,000 ₪ לא כולל מע"מ"; "כל משמרת עריכה נוספת תעלה עוד 1,400 ₪ ל-8 שעות עבודה"; and the cost can reach ₪10,000 or more. Source: Midrag. https://www.midrag.co.il/Content/Tip/11772. The List's freelancer price list (03.2024) gives a reels/TikTok shoot day plus editing of about 6 videos at "2000-3000 ₪" for a junior and "3000-4500 ₪" for a senior. https://www.thelist.co.il/post/המחירון-של-מקצועני-הליסט-סושיאל-וידאו-וגרפיקה-לדיגיטל-2024

8. **A single reel from an Israeli freelancer costs ₪190–800.** SmartPush charges "₪ 190 לסרטון רילס" for basic editing with "כתוביות בסיסי לא מונפש", ₪290 for animated captions and ₪450 for the premium tier with "תסריטים". Kanmedia: "ייצור רילס או וידאו – לרוב עולה 300–800₪ לתוכן בודד". Other quotes seen only in search snippets: ₪270 per reel, 2 reels of up to 30 seconds for ₪400 + VAT, and ₪300–1,000 per reel. Klipi's ₪29–59 is about 5 to 25 times cheaper. https://smartpush.co.il/מחיר-של-רילס-כל-מה-שצריך-לדעת-או-תפסו-אצ/ and https://kanmedia.co.il/כמה-עולה-ניהול-רשתות-חברתיות-לעסק-קטן-ב/

9. **Monthly social-media management costs ₪1,500–8,000.** Kanmedia: "פרילנסר עולה 1,500–3,500₪/חודש" and "סוכנות עולה 3,500–8,000₪/חודש". Chen Kaufman's price list starts at "₪ 1900 לחודש (לפני מע״מ)" for "2 פוסטים בשבוע + סטורי" with "עריכת סרטונים קצרים", then ₪3,500 and ₪5,500. For a פטור with about ₪30K yearly turnover, even ₪1,900 a month is impossible. https://chen-seo.co.il/instagram-pricing-table/

10. **Owners pay for posts and get no results.** Kanmedia: "רוב עסקים קטנים משלמים על פוסטים – לא על תוצאות"; "רוב הפרילנסרים מוכרים לך נוכחות ומציגים אותה כניהול"; "סוכנות גדולה שמשלמת לה 6,000₪ לחודש – עשויה להקצות לך 3–4 שעות עבודה בפועל בשבוע". MATI Jerusalem, a state-backed small-business support centre, wrote on 02.08.2026: "הרבה בעלי עסקים קטנים מרגישים שהם 'שורפים' תקציב בלי לראות תוצאה". https://www.mati.org.il/2026/08/02/facebbok_guide/

11. **Paid ads start small, so owners need cheap creative.** MATI: "לעסק קטן אפשר להתחיל מ-20–50 ₪ ליום ולגדול בהדרגה", and it lists "תמונה או וידאו" as the ad creative. Other Israeli pages put a starting media budget at ₪1,500–3,000 a month. That figure was seen in snippets only. https://www.mati.org.il/2026/08/02/facebbok_guide/

12. **One-person freelancers are a reliability risk.** Israeli agency-versus-freelancer guides keep warning that a freelancer who is sick or on holiday leaves the account untouched, and that a campaign stops. Kanmedia says a sick or overloaded freelancer can stop a campaign. This evidence is weaker: I found no first-hand post saying "the freelancer disappeared". Sources: bidul.co.il, boostit.co.il and tomana.co.il, all from search snippets.

13. **Hebrew text in video tools is often broken.** Forum reports, seen in search snippets: in Shotcut, Hebrew text previews correctly but exports with the letters reversed. Adobe After Effects users say RTL support "is not completed", and Hebrew text animation ran in reverse until AE 2023. On Figma, designers do not notice reversed Hebrew letters. I found no CapCut- or Canva-specific complaint in the time available. https://forum.shotcut.org/t/supporting-right-to-left-text/21859 and https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/rtl-support-right-to-left-languages-is-not-completed-hebrew-and-possibly-other-mena-issues/m-p/13229509

14. **Small businesses lag on AI, and most non-users think it is "not for us".** Israel's Central Bureau of Statistics (הלמ"ס) found in June 2025 that about 28% of businesses use AI and 17% pay for it. Among small businesses with up to 50 jobs, about 16% pay, against 29% of large businesses. 71% of businesses that do not plan to use AI said "פעילותו הכלכלית של העסק אינה רלוונטית לטכנולוגיה זו". Source: CBS press release 216/2025, 15.07.2025. https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/mediarelease/DocLib/2025/216/31_25_216b.pdf

## 2. SO WHAT FOR KLIPI

- **Put the price comparison on the home page.** Example: "רילס אצל פרילנסר: ₪190–800 וכמה ימים. בקליפי: מ-₪29, תוך דקות". The numbers in findings 7–9 are public and checkable. A פטור turning over about ₪30K a year cannot pay ₪1,900 a month, but can pay ₪29.
- **Lead with "no filming, no time, no ideas" and keep "AI" in the background.** Camera shyness, missing ideas and lack of time are the pains owners name (findings 5 and 6). 71% of non-adopters think AI "is not relevant to my business". Suggested headlines: "סרטון לעסק בלי להצטלם", "3 רעיונות מוכנים – אתם רק בוחרים", "מוכן בזמן שהקפה מתקרר". The three Hebrew creative directions are the strongest feature here, because they answer "what do I say".
- **Build for WhatsApp first, Instagram second.** WhatsApp reaches 99% of Israelis and is the main channel for many owners. Make 9:16 the default and add a "שתפו לסטטוס בוואטסאפ" button. Keep exported files small enough to send on WhatsApp. Offer a short cut-down version for status. Klipi should not depend on WhatsApp mass messaging for its own marketing, because of the October 2025 blocking wave (finding 4).
- **Sell consistency, not one video.** Owners fail at posting regularly ("2 videos a year"). Add a monthly pack, for example 4 videos plus the holiday calendar for about ₪99–149 a month. That is still about 10 times cheaper than a freelancer at ₪1,500 or more, and it makes the existing holiday templates and brand kit pay off.
- **Make correct Hebrew a visible promise and test it hard.** Reversed Hebrew letters are a known failure in video tools (finding 13). Show a Hebrew before/after on the landing page ("עברית שלא מתהפכת"). Test punctuation, numbers inside Hebrew text, mixed Hebrew/English brand names and phone numbers in every template before launch.
- **Answer "I paid and got nothing".** Owners distrust paying for presence without results (finding 10). Add a clear call to action to the end of every video: phone, WhatsApp link, address, opening hours. Add a short "how to post this and what to expect" tip. Do not promise sales. Instant delivery plus the 2 included fix rounds answers the "freelancer disappeared" fear (finding 12).
- **Sell the video as cheap ad creative.** Owners who start Meta ads at ₪20–50 a day need a video, not a ₪3,000 production. Offer a 9:16 version for Reels/Stories ads and a 1:1 or 16:9 version for Feed ads.
- **Before card payment goes live, the owner needs a business registration.** The owner has no תיק עוסק yet. Opening an עוסק פטור costs nothing and puts him in the same group as his customers. Without it he cannot issue receipts.

## Gaps (not found within the time limit)
- No national breakdown of small businesses by sector (food, beauty and so on). CBS has it only at the level of "personal services" tables, which I did not reach.
- No first-hand Facebook-group or Reddit posts from Israeli owners. The voices above come from articles and price pages, some of them written by sellers of marketing services.
- No Fiverr price in shekels for a Hebrew video editor. The one Hebrew editor gig found quotes only after a brief.
- No CapCut- or Canva-specific complaint about broken Hebrew.
