# Target audience and customer pains: synthesis (22 Oct)

Sources: israel-smb.md, competitors.md and segments.md in this folder.

## Who
- Primary: Israeli micro-businesses that sell locally to consumers and have photos.
  - Order of priority: food (bakeries, cafés, pizzerias), then beauty/nails/barbers, then event suppliers, then retail.
  - Secondary: fitness, real-estate agents, courses and coaches.
  - Last: clinics, because of trust and regulation.
- Size of the market: 558K businesses under the micro ceiling (52% of VAT businesses); 430K are עוסק פטור. Typical turnover is ₪30-50K a year.
- Channels: WhatsApp 99%, Facebook 88%, Instagram 81% (67% use it daily), TikTok 57%.

## Pains
1. No time: marketing happens at 11pm.
2. Don't know what to say or where to start (37% of non-users say this). Shy on camera. Few posts.
3. Price of the alternatives:
   - a filmed promo video: ₪3-4K;
   - a freelancer reel: ₪190-800;
   - social management: ₪1.5-8K a month.
4. Paid and got nothing; freelancers are unreliable.
5. Existing AI tools:
   - subscriptions with surprise renewals and no refunds;
   - credits burned on failed videos;
   - watermarks and slow renders;
   - stock footage that isn't their business;
   - broken RTL/Hebrew.
6. Fear that AI looks cheap or fake, and customer backlash:
   - the bagel-shop case;
   - 62% reject AI people/voices even when disclosed;
   - a person with AI is trusted by 48%, AI alone by 13%.
7. Creatives go stale in 2-4 weeks (days on TikTok), so they need a steady flow.

## What Klipi already answers
- Real photos, no avatars and no stock.
- 3 ready directions, which answers "what to say".
- 2 fix rounds.
- One-off low price.
- Hebrew-first.
- Holiday templates, posts, menu screen.

## Recommendations (priority order)
1. Home-page messaging:
   - "בלי לצלם, בלי לכתוב, בלי מנוי";
   - "מהתמונות האמיתיות שלכם, בלי אוכל מומצא ובלי אנשים מומצאים";
   - a careful, truthful price anchor against typical freelancer and studio prices.
2. Guarantees:
   - one-off payment;
   - no auto-renew, ever;
   - a failed video costs nothing;
   - an instant receipt (when payments launch).
3. WhatsApp first:
   - a share-to-status button;
   - small files;
   - a WhatsApp CTA end card;
   - 9:16 as the default.
4. A short 15 s cut for status and Reels.
5. Prepaid packs (no auto-renew) plus a "time for a fresh video" reminder after about 3 weeks, only with marketing consent.
6. Trust:
   - a gallery of real before/after (raw photos → video);
   - "אתם בוחרים, אתם מאשרים";
   - an optional AI-disclosure end card that the customer controls.
7. Hebrew quality as a promise ("עברית שלא מתהפכת"), with tests for mixed text.
8. An honest render time, plus a notification when the video is ready.
9. Go-to-market: the brother's bakery as case study #1, then nearby food and beauty businesses.
