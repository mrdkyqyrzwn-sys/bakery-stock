# Competitors angle: what users hate and love about existing video-ad tools

Scope: Creatify, InVideo AI, Pictory, CapCut, HeyGen, Synthesia, Arcads, plus Hebrew/RTL reports from video editors. Time-boxed at about 8 minutes.
Sources are marked **[read]** when I opened the page myself, or **[snippet]** when the evidence comes only from a search-result summary and should be checked before anyone quotes it publicly.

## 1. FINDINGS

1. **Billing and cancellation are the top complaint about AI ad tools, more than output quality.** Creatify has a TrustScore of 3.7 from 842 reviews. Two quotes: "there are no refunds, even for a completely unused billing period." (Vladimir K., 18 Oct 2026) and "This wasn't made clear at the point of cancellation." (Zoe Basil, 28 Sep 2026). Snippets from other Trustpilot pages also describe a monthly plan billed as annual (€193.80) and a $588 yearly renewal charged with no reminder email. [read] https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/creatify.ai ; [snippet] https://de.trustpilot.com/review/creatify.ai

2. **InVideo AI has the same problem, and worse.** Its TrustScore is 2.9 from 908 reviews. Two quotes: "Charges still happening after 3 attempts to cancel the service (with confirmation)." (Andre Bothma, Apr 2026) and "If you've only used the service for 5 minutes and one test video, they should grant a refund without hesitation." [read] https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/ai.invideo.io?page=10

3. **Credits run out, and failed attempts still use them up.** Two InVideo quotes: "I already used 94% of my monthly credits." and "Unable to provide a proper video after expending extra credits." A HeyGen reviewer on G2 (a software review site) wrote: "It takes some pratice to learn how to get the best out of HeyGen and therefore credits." [read] InVideo Trustpilot (above) ; [snippet] https://g2.com/products/heygen/reviews?page=2

4. **Stock footage often doesn't match the business.** For Pictory, 94 reviews say the "AI occasionally selects irrelevant stock clips that require manual replacement". A G2 reviewer wrote: "Sometimes it picks a clip that has nothing to do with the sentence." A Capterra reviewer says images of "certain, specific people or companies are not available in the Pictory library". An InVideo reviewer said the results "looked like terrible presentations". [read] https://tooliverse.ai/tools/pictory ; [snippet] https://www.capterra.com/p/229347/Pictory/reviews?page=3

5. **AI avatars look fake or are unstable.** A Creatify reviewer wrote: "The model's face changed into a different person while she was moving" (12 Sep 2026). A comparison site says Creatify presenters "do not hold the same face between generations". [read] Creatify Trustpilot ; [snippet] https://icon.com/blog/creatify-alternatives

6. **Paywall and watermark creep is the main anger at CapCut, the free tool most people fall back on.** An analysis of US Google Play reviews (Jul–Sep 2026) found 900 of 2,881 substantial negative reviews (31%) mention Pro, a paywall or a subscription. Of those, 70 say export was refused at the last step and 54 mention the watermark. One quote: "There's also a bold "capcut" sign on the entire screen that can only be removed with a subscription." Saudi App Store reviews say: "Almost all the effects are pro!", "the problem is the watermark and the pro effects" and "why is the Pro and Standard really expensive?" [read] https://unstar.app/blog/capcut-everything-is-pro-now-cant-export-reviews-2026 ; [read] https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/capcut-video-editor/id1500855883?l=ar

7. **Renders are slow.** For Pictory, 76 reviews say "Video rendering times can be significantly slow". An InVideo reviewer said it "took over 30 minutes to generate anything". A HeyGen reviewer on G2 said processing sometimes took more than 30 minutes. [read] Tooliverse, InVideo Trustpilot ; [snippet] G2 HeyGen

8. **Fixes can cost extra.** Reviews of Arcads say some users were charged extra for script edits, "even minor ones like adjusting pronunciation". A comparison article puts Creatify's free plan at 10 credits a month with a watermark, then $39 a month (Starter) or $99 a month (Pro). [snippet] https://icon.com/blog/creatify-alternatives , https://creatify.ai/review

9. **Hebrew is weak, mostly when you type text or animate it.** One page, which sells a competing captions tool so it is biased, says: "CapCut's caption editor is designed for left-to-right input" and "When typing or pasting Hebrew text, cursor behaviour and character placement can behave unexpectedly." It also says CapCut's auto-captions "work reliably only in English and a small number of major languages." [read] https://caption-x.com/capcut-captions/hebrew

10. **The Hebrew and Arabic complaints I found are real and specific, but they come from video editors, not the AI ad tools.**
    - **After Effects** (Israeli users, 2022): "text animators still work left to right, with right to left text." and "RTL users will be forced to stay in previous versions without the ability to upgrade until you fix this." Adobe fixed this only in 2023.
    - **Shotcut** (2020): Hebrew text was reversed in the preview but correct in the export, so what you see is not what you get.
    - **CapCut App Store** (snippet): an Arabic user says typed text "comes out all messy and backwards". They typed it in another app and pasted it in as an overlay, which lost the text animations.

    I found **no Hebrew-specific reviews of Creatify, InVideo, Pictory, Arcads or HeyGen** on the review sites. Hebrew speakers barely show up in their reviews, which suggests these tools don't really serve them. [read] https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-ideas/rtl-support-right-to-left-languages-is-not-completed-hebrew-and-possibly-other-mena-issues/m-p/13229509 ; [read] https://forum.shotcut.org/t/supporting-right-to-left-text/21859 ; [snippet] https://apple.co/3Tnw3tL

11. **What people love and pay for: speed, ease and looking "bigger".**
    - Creatify: "The interface is incredibly easy to use and allows me to create ads for my business with total ease." (Oct 2026)
    - Synthesia on G2: "makes my small business marketing appear as if we have a huge budget."
    - HeyGen on G2: "go from a script to a finished video in minutes".
    - The top Pictory praise is speed (184 reviews), the stock library (156) and automatic captions (142). [read] Creatify Trustpilot, Tooliverse ; [snippet] https://g2.com/products/synthesia/reviews?page=2 , https://g2.com/products/heygen/reviews?page=2

12. **Price is a reason to buy.** A Creatify reviewer wrote: "The fact that its half the price of other UGC ad creative websites out there really sold it for me." (UGC means videos that look like a customer filmed them.) Unhappy users go back to the free option: "I'd recommend saving your money and using CapCut or a similar alternative." (Oct 2026). [read] Creatify Trustpilot

13. **CapCut is still loved despite the anger.** It is rated 4.5 from about 153K ratings on the Saudi App Store. The complaints are about paywalls, not the editor itself. CapCut is the real default Klipi competes with. [read] App Store SA

## 2. SO WHAT FOR KLIPI

- **Make "no subscription" a headline.** Something like "תשלום חד-פעמי לסרטון. בלי מנוי, בלי חידוש אוטומטי, בלי קרדיטים." This is the biggest pain in findings 1–3 and 6. When card payments launch, never auto-renew, and send a receipt right away.
- **Promise that a failed video costs nothing.** If a render fails or the customer hates all three directions, they shouldn't pay, or Klipi redoes it. Put this on the pricing page next to "2 סבבי תיקונים כלולים". Competitors charge for edits and failed attempts (findings 3 and 8).
- **Sell "your real photos, not stock" as the main difference.** Show a side-by-side: a generic stock bakery next to the real "מאפה הבית" photos. This answers finding 4 directly. A suggested line: "רואים את העסק שלך, לא סרטון מאגר."
- **Skip AI avatars or talking presenters for now.** That's where the "fake face" complaints come from (finding 5). Real photos with Hebrew text and music are safer and fit a bakery better.
- **Treat good Hebrew as the product, and test it in the exported file, not the preview.** Test mixed text like "חלה ב-₪29", English brand names, phone numbers, punctuation at the end of a line and animated text. These are exactly what breaks in other tools (findings 9–10). A short demo with perfect Hebrew motion text is a strong proof point. A suggested line: "עברית אמיתית, מימין לשמאל, בלי אותיות הפוכות".
- **Be clear about watermarks.** Paid videos should have no watermark at all. If free coupon videos carry a small mark, say so before the customer starts (finding 6).
- **Show an honest render time and notify when it's ready**, by WhatsApp or email, so people don't stare at a spinner for 30 minutes (finding 7).
- **Price against the real alternatives.** ₪29 is less than a month of Creatify ($39) and needs no learning curve, unlike CapCut. Copy idea: "זול מחודש אחד של כלי מנוי, ובלי ללמוד עריכה". People who praise these tools mention "easy" and "minutes", so keep Klipi to three choices and no timeline editor (findings 11–12).
